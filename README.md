# Trig Sub Drill

Calc 2 study app for the three trig substitutions. Four modes:

- **Tap to reveal**: the table with blanks you uncover one at a time
- **Clue round**: get one box of a row, type the other three
- **Blank table**: write the whole table from memory, then check it
- **Cheat sheet**: every row, how to spot it, and why the sub works

| Radical form | Sub | Identity | dx |
|---|---|---|---|
| √(a² − x²) | x = a sin θ | 1 − sin²θ = cos²θ | dx = a cos θ dθ |
| √(a² + x²) | x = a tan θ | 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ | dx = a sec²θ dθ |
| √(x² − a²) | x = a sec θ | sec²θ − 1 = tan²θ | dx = a sec θ tan θ dθ |

## On your phone

Open **https://sahltyy.github.io/trigSub-memory-drill/** in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. Open it once while online. After that it works with no connection.

The site is set to stay out of search results.
